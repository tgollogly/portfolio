Free Apple Wallet — 5 minutes

Apple blocks unsigned passes, so use the free Pass2U Wallet app (it signs the pass for you):

  1. Tap Add to Apple Wallet (free) above → install Pass2U Wallet from the App Store.
  2. Open Pass2U → tap + → choose Store Card or Generic.
  3. Fill in: Thomas Gollogly, Self-taught Developer, thomas@tgollogly.dev, , tgollogly.dev.
  4. Set colour to blue (#2f39c9) if you want it to match this card.
  5. Tap Add to Wallet — done. Your card is in the Wallet app.
